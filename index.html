The quick answer

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product list used to organize shopping finds for comparison. It may include product links, categories, listed prices, sizes and notes. Each list has its own scope; a spreadsheet entry alone does not verify a seller, confirm availability or establish product authenticity.

Oopbuy shopping guide

Build a shortlist you can use.

A useful list records what you have checked and what still needs an answer.

01

Choose a clear category

Start an Oopbuy product list with one purpose: everyday sneakers, a lightweight jacket or accessories for a specific outfit. Save the exact listing and variant so similar photos do not blur together.

02

Compare the evidence

For Oopbuy spreadsheet finds, record measurements, stated materials and available photos. Keep seller claims separate from details you can inspect. Mark missing information instead of guessing.

03

Review the full cost

Compare the item price alongside delivery estimates, potential fees and return conditions. Recheck the listing before ordering because prices, variants and availability can change.

Fit / construction / details

Check each category differently.

Oopbuy shoes spreadsheet

Keep footwear comparisons focused on fit and construction. A familiar size number is a starting point, but it does not replace the listing’s own measurements.

  • Compare the seller’s size chart and available length measurements.
  • Inspect outsole, upper, lining and closure details in the photos.
  • Record the exact colorway and whether packaging changes shipping estimates.

Oopbuy clothing spreadsheet

For T-shirts, hoodies, jackets and sets, compare garment measurements with clothing that already fits you. Check whether the figures describe the garment or the wearer.

  • Record chest width, shoulder width, sleeve length and overall length.
  • For pants and shorts, check waist, rise, inseam and leg opening.
  • For headwear and jewelry, check circumference, dimensions and fastening type.

Before you decide

Questions, answered clearly.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product list used to organize shopping finds for comparison. It may include product links, categories, listed prices, sizes and notes. Each list has its own scope, and inclusion does not verify a seller or product.

Is this an official Oopbuy website?

No. This is an independent shopping guide with links to CAFANS. It does not provide an Oopbuy login, official inventory feed or downloadable spreadsheet.

How do I use an Oopbuy product list?

Choose a category, open the product listing and record the exact variant, measurements, listed price and seller information. Compare delivery costs, available QC photos and return conditions before deciding.

Does a spreadsheet price include shipping?

Do not assume that it does. Check whether the listed amount includes domestic delivery, international shipping, service fees and any applicable taxes or duties.

Do QC photos prove that a branded item is authentic?

No. QC photos may help you inspect visible details such as measurements, color and stitching, but they do not independently establish authenticity.

How should I choose clothing and shoe sizes?

Compare the listing’s measurements with an item you already own. Check the units and measuring method. For footwear, use the seller’s size chart and available length measurements rather than relying only on a familiar size label.
